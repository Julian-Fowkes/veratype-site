1. Overview

Veratype ("we", "our", "us") provides a Chrome extension that monitors writing behaviour in Google Docs to generate a behavioral integrity score. This policy explains what data the extension collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

The short version: We collect behavioural metadata about writing sessions — typing rhythm, paste events, deletions. We never store the actual content of what you type. Your raw keystrokes are never recorded or transmitted.

2. What we collect

When the Veratype extension is active during a Google Docs session, we collect the following behavioural metadata:

  • Keystroke categories — the type of key pressed (alphabetic, numeric, symbol, delete, enter, space, arrow, or other). We do not record which specific key was pressed.
  • Inter-keystroke intervals (IKI) — the time in milliseconds between consecutive key presses. This is used to identify natural human typing rhythm.
  • Paste events — the number of paste actions and the character count of pasted content. We do not capture the pasted text itself.
  • Deletion count — the number of delete/backspace key presses in a session.
  • DOM mutation signals — aggregate counts of characters inserted or deleted in the document editor, used as a cross-check against keystroke data.
  • Session metadata — a randomly generated session ID, session start time, session duration, the Google Docs document ID, and the document title.

This data is associated with a user account created by the educator who installed and configured Veratype for their institution.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • The text you type — we never record the content of your writing.
  • The specific keys you press — only broad categories (e.g. "alphabetic key") are recorded.
  • The content of pasted text — only the character count is captured.
  • Browsing history, other tabs, or any activity outside of Google Docs.
  • Passwords, form data, or any other sensitive input.
  • Camera, microphone, or any device sensor data.

4. How we use your data

We use the behavioural metadata we collect solely to:

  • Calculate a behavioral integrity score for each writing session.
  • Generate a flag summary that educators can review in the Veratype dashboard.
  • Improve the accuracy of our scoring algorithms over time using aggregated, anonymised data.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the integrity scoring service.

5. Who sees your data

Session data is visible to:

  • The educator or institution that installed and configured Veratype — they can see the integrity scores and flag summaries for sessions conducted under their account.
  • Veratype staff — only when required for technical support, debugging, or security investigations, and only to the minimum extent necessary.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for any commercial purpose.

6. Data storage and security

All session data is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure, specifically Amazon RDS (PostgreSQL) hosted in the us-east-1 region.

We apply the following security measures:

  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Database access is restricted by IAM roles and security groups — no public internet access to the database.
  • Application-level authentication is required to access any session data.

7. Data retention

Session data is retained for as long as the associated educator account is active. If an account is deleted, all associated session data is permanently removed within 30 days.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see Section 11).

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 11. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's privacy

Veratype is intended for use in educational settings and may process data relating to students, including those under the age of 13. Veratype is deployed by educational institutions, who are responsible for obtaining any necessary parental consent in compliance with applicable law (including COPPA in the United States and equivalent regulations elsewhere).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children directly. Session data is collected under the authority of the educator or institution account holder.
