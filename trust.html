For Schools

Compliance, security, and student privacy commitments

1. Student privacy commitments

Veratype is built around three core privacy guarantees:

  • No content captured. The extension never reads, stores, or transmits the text students write. We observe only behavioural metadata — timing, paste counts, editing patterns.
  • No plaintext keys. Key-pair timing data is stored only as irreversible one-way hashes. Neither Veratype nor any third party can reconstruct which keys were pressed.
  • No activity tracking outside the editor. We record when the writing tab loses and regains focus (to measure session continuity), but never capture which site or application was used in the meantime.

2. Regulatory posture

  • FERPA: Veratype operates as a "school official" under FERPA, processing student data solely at the direction of and for the benefit of the educational institution. We use data only for the purposes defined in our agreement with the school.
  • COPPA: Consent for students under 13 flows through the educational institution. The school is responsible for obtaining any necessary parental consent; Veratype does not collect personal information directly from children without institutional authorization.
  • State student-data-privacy laws: We are prepared to sign Data Privacy Agreements (DPAs) consistent with state-specific requirements. Contact us for DPA negotiation.

Note: These are operational commitments reflecting how we build and operate Veratype. They are not legal certifications. We encourage schools to review our privacy policy and request a DPA if required by their jurisdiction.

3. Security measures

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the extension, our API, and the educator dashboard is encrypted using TLS.
  • Encryption at rest: Database storage is encrypted at rest using AWS-managed keys.
  • IAM-restricted private database: The PostgreSQL database is hosted in a private subnet with no public internet access, secured by IAM roles and security groups.
  • Session-cookie authentication: Educator and student sessions are authenticated via secure, HTTP-only cookies.
  • Hashed tokens: Sensitive tokens (password reset, verification) are stored as one-way hashes.

5. Data deletion

When a user's data is deleted — by request or by account deletion — all identifiable data (sessions, raw behavioural events, scores, typing-rhythm baselines, and account information) is permanently removed. We retain only de-identified statistical feature summaries that contain no user, document, class, or school references and cannot be re-linked to any individual.

Deletion requests are honored within 30 days. Districts may request bulk deletion of all student data at contract end.
