For students

Your teacher has asked you to use Veratype. Here is what it does and how to set it up.

You need a class code.

Your teacher gives you an 8-character code for your class. You register with that code and your student email. Without a code you cannot register, and nothing is recorded.

Set it up

  1. Add Veratype to Chrome. It is a free Chrome extension. A small window opens when it is installed.
  2. Read what it collects, then enter your class code and student email. Nothing is recorded before you have registered.
  3. Open the link we email you, in the same browser. That links the extension to your account, already in your class.

Have your class code ready.

Add to Chrome

What it records

  • How you type, not what you type. The timing of key presses and the kind of key (letter, digit, delete, shortcut). Not which keys you press, and not your text.
  • Pastes, copies and cuts, as counts. How many characters, never the content.
  • When the tab was hidden. Not where you went.
  • The document's title and ID, your student email, and the class you choose.

What you control

  • Only documents for a class. Mark a document "Not for a class" in the Veratype panel and nothing is recorded in it.
  • Who sees it. The teacher of each class you choose for a document, and their school's Veratype administrator. A class keeps a document once you have chosen it.
  • Your data. It is deleted automatically after 12 months, and you can ask for it to be deleted sooner by emailing privacy@veratype.ai.

About the score

Veratype gives each document a score for how it was produced. A score is an indicator, not evidence: any decision about your work has to be made by a person, and a score alone is not grounds for one. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
