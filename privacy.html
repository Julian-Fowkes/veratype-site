1. Overview

Veratype ("we", "our", "us") provides a Chrome extension that monitors writing behaviour in Google Docs and Microsoft Word Online to generate a behavioral integrity score. This policy explains what data the extension collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

The short version: We collect behavioural metadata about writing sessions: typing rhythm, paste events, deletions. We never store the actual content of what you type. Your raw keystrokes are never recorded or transmitted.

2. What we collect

When the Veratype extension is active during a Google Docs or Word Online session, we collect the following behavioural metadata:

  • Keystroke categories: the type of key pressed (alphabetic, numeric, symbol, delete, enter, space, arrow, or other). We do not record which specific key was pressed.
  • Inter-keystroke intervals (IKI): the time in milliseconds between consecutive key presses. This is used to identify natural human typing rhythm.
  • Paste events: the number of paste actions and the character count of pasted content. We do not capture the pasted text itself.
  • Deletion count: the number of delete/backspace key presses in a session.
  • DOM mutation signals: aggregate counts of characters inserted or deleted in the document editor, used as a cross-check against keystroke data.
  • Session metadata: a randomly generated session ID, session start time, session duration, the document ID (from Google Docs or Word Online), and the document title.
  • Key-pair timing (summarised and hashed): for common pairs of keys, how many times the pair occurred in a session and how quickly, stored under a one-way-hashed identifier of the pair. It is a summary, not a sequence: it does not record the order in which keys were pressed. Used to build each student's personal typing-rhythm baseline.
  • Copy and cut events: that a copy or cut happened, so that moving your own text within a document is not mistaken for pasting from elsewhere. The comparison is made inside your browser; the copied text is never transmitted.
  • Unexplained document growth (Google Docs): a count of lines that appeared without keyboard or clipboard input (for example voice typing). It is shown to the educator as context and is not part of the score.
  • Modifier key usage: whether Ctrl, Cmd, Shift, or Alt was held during a keystroke (used to detect copy-paste-heavy workflows).
  • Tab visibility periods: timestamps for when the Google Docs / Word Online tab was hidden and when it became visible again. We record only that the tab lost focus — never which site or application was used in the meantime.
  • Timezone offset: your browser's UTC offset, used to interpret session start times in local time.
  • Account information: the email address a student provides at registration, consent timestamp, and class enrollment (class join codes entered by the student).

This data is associated with the account the student creates when registering the extension, and with any class the student joins via a class code.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • The text you type. We never record the content of your writing.
  • The specific keys you press. Keystrokes are recorded as broad categories (e.g. 'alphabetic key'), and key-pair rhythm is kept only as a per-session summary under one-way hashes (how often and how fast, in no order) — neither can be used to reconstruct what was written.
  • The content of pasted text. Only the character count is captured.
  • Browsing history, the content or identity of other tabs, or what you do outside of Google Docs and Word Online. (We do record when the writing tab loses and regains focus — see Section 2 — but never where you went.)
  • Passwords, form data, or any other sensitive input.
  • Camera, microphone, or any device sensor data.

4. How we use your data

We use the behavioural metadata we collect solely to:

  • Calculate a behavioral integrity score for each writing session.
  • Generate a flag summary that educators can review in the Veratype dashboard.
  • Improve the accuracy of our scoring algorithms over time using aggregated, anonymised data.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the integrity scoring service.

5. Who sees your data

Session data is visible to:

  • The educator of each class you chose for a document (and their school's Veratype administrator). They can see the integrity scores and flag summaries for that document — not your other documents. A class keeps a document once you have chosen it: if you later choose another class for the same document, that class is added and the first is not removed, and each of them sees all of your writing sessions for that document.
  • Veratype staff, only when required for technical support, debugging, or security investigations, and only to the minimum extent necessary.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for any commercial purpose.

6. Data storage and security

All session data is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure, specifically Amazon RDS (PostgreSQL) hosted in the us-east-1 region.

We apply the following security measures:

  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Database access is restricted by IAM roles and security groups, with no public internet access to the database.
  • Application-level authentication is required to access any session data.

Service providers

  • Amazon Web Services (us-east-1): hosting, database (RDS PostgreSQL), and AI processing. Scores, flags and session counts (never raw keystroke streams, and never the student's name, email or document title) are processed by Amazon Bedrock to generate educator-facing report text and weekly digests.
  • Resend: transactional email delivery (account verification, educator invites, password resets, weekly digests, contact-form forwarding). Resend processes the recipient email address and message content.
  • Cloudflare: hosting of this website. No student data.
  • Google Fonts: web fonts on our pages; Google receives the visitor's IP address.

7. Data retention

Session data (behavioural events, scores and the summaries built from them) is deleted automatically 12 months after the session. Your account is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. When a user's data is deleted — by request or by account deletion — all identifiable data (sessions, raw behavioural events, scores, typing-rhythm baselines, and account information) is permanently removed from the live service. We retain only de-identified statistical feature summaries that contain no user, document, class, or school references and cannot be re-linked to any individual; these are used solely to improve scoring accuracy. Encrypted database backups are kept for up to 12 months and then expire. Deletion requests are honored within 30 days (see Section 11).

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 11. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's privacy

Veratype is intended for use in educational settings and may process data relating to students, including those under the age of 13. Veratype is deployed by educational institutions, who are responsible for obtaining any necessary parental consent in compliance with applicable law (including COPPA in the United States and equivalent regulations elsewhere).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children directly. Session data is collected under the authority of the educator or institution account holder.
