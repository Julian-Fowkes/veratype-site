How it works

Simple for educators.
Invisible to students.

Veratype runs quietly in the background — no logins for students, no changes to their workflow.

Step 01
🧩

Install the extension

Add Veratype to Chrome in seconds. It activates automatically whenever a student opens a Google Doc.

Step 02
⌨️

Session monitoring begins

Veratype tracks keystrokes, paste events, deletions, and inter-keystroke intervals — the behavioural fingerprint of genuine writing.

Step 03
📊

Review the integrity score

After a session, educators see a score and flag summary. Authentic, Review, or High Risk — backed by real behavioral data.

Features

Everything an educator needs.

No complex setup. No disruption. Just clear signal on authentic work.

⌨️

Keystroke analysis

Tracks typing rhythm and inter-keystroke intervals — a pattern that's nearly impossible to fake and hard to replicate with AI tools.

📋

Paste detection

Catches large paste events instantly. A document written in 3 minutes with 7 pastes tells a very different story than one typed over an hour.

🟢

Three-tier scoring

Sessions are scored Authentic, Review, or High Risk based on behavioral signals — clear enough to act on, nuanced enough to be fair.

🔒

Privacy-first design

No raw keystrokes are ever stored. Veratype records behavioural metadata only — what you typed is never seen by anyone.

📈

Educator dashboard

Review all sessions in one place. Sort by risk level, filter by student or document, and click through to full flag summaries.

⚡

Zero friction

Students open Google Docs as normal. Veratype works silently in the background — no accounts to create, no workflow to change.
