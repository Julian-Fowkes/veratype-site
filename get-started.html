Set up your school

Tell us about your school. We create it and send you an invite.

Veratype is set up school by school: we create your school, you invite your teachers, and each class gets a code that students use to register. Nothing is recorded for a student who is not in a class.

We use these details to reply to you and to set up your school, and delete them after 12 months. See the privacy policy.

What happens next

  1. We create your school and email you an invite. A person reads every request.
  2. You set a password, create a class and get its code. If you are the school's administrator, you can invite other teachers too.
  3. Students install the extension and register with that code. From then on you see how each document was written. See what students see.

What it costs

Veratype is free for a teacher and their classes, and free for students. Pilots are free. School-wide and district licences are arranged with us: say so in the form and we will talk.

Before you ask

Compliance, security and student privacy are covered on For schools, and the full policy is the privacy policy. For anything else, email support@veratype.ai.
