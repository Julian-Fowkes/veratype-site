How it works

Simple for you.
Private for them.

Veratype runs quietly while students write naturally in Google Docs or Word Online. No logins, no disruption — and we never read their work.

1

Install the extension

Add Veratype to Chrome in seconds. It activates automatically when a student opens Google Docs or Word Online — no student setup required.

2

The algorithm goes to work

Veratype's algorithm scores 13 behavioural signals — paste patterns, editing flow, session timing, and more — to build a fingerprint of how the document was created. It never sees what students actually type.

3

You see the integrity score

A clear score — Authentic, Review, or High Risk — tells you what you need to know. No guesswork, no reading through drafts looking for red flags.

Features

Privacy is the foundation.
Not an afterthought.

Academic integrity tools shouldn't come at the cost of student privacy. Every feature is built with both in mind.

🔒

Student privacy, protected

Veratype never stores what students type. Not a single word. Only behavioural patterns — rhythm, timing, editing flow — are analyzed by our algorithm. The actual writing stays private.

🧠

Algorithm-powered scoring

Our algorithm scores 13 behavioural signals — editing flow, paste patterns, session timing, and more — to build a picture of how a document was written. It adapts to each student's natural baseline over time.

📋

Paste detection

A document typed over an hour looks very different from one assembled in 3 minutes with 7 pastes. Veratype sees the difference so you don't have to guess.

🟢

Fair, three-tier scoring

Authentic, Review, or High Risk. Clear enough to act on, nuanced enough to give students the benefit of the doubt. No binary pass/fail judgments.

📄

Google Docs & Word Online

Whether your school runs on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, Veratype works. One extension, both platforms, same privacy guarantees.

⚡

Zero friction for students

Students just open their document and write. No accounts, no setup, no change to their workflow. They focus on their work — Veratype handles the rest.
