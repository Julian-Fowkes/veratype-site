HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. No disruption.

Students write normally. You get a score. Nobody's workflow changes.

01

Students install the extension

One click from the Chrome Web Store, register with an email, done. It turns on automatically in Google Docs and Word Online.

02

They write. We measure behaviour.

Typing rhythm, paste frequency, editing patterns, session duration — 18 signals scored in the background. We measure how the document was produced, not what it says.

03

You get a clear answer

Each session is scored: Authentic, Questionable, or Inauthentic. You decide what to do with it. No guesswork, no reading through drafts.

FEATURES

What it does.
What it doesn't.

The short version: we see timing and patterns. We never see content.

We never see the words

Not a character. Not a sentence. We store key categories ("alphabetic key"), timing between keys as irreversible hashes, and paste counts. The actual writing never leaves the browser.

18 signals, personalised baselines

Paste ratio, typing rhythm, editing distribution, typing-rhythm baselines, and more. Scoring adapts to each student's own patterns over time — so fast typists aren't penalised for being fast.

Paste detection

An essay typed over an hour looks nothing like one assembled from 7 paste events in 3 minutes. We track paste count, size, and timing.

Three tiers, not pass/fail

Authentic, Questionable, or Inauthentic. Most students score Authentic. The middle tier is there because ambiguity is real — you decide whether to follow up.

Google Docs & Word Online

Works in both. Google Workspace schools, Microsoft 365 schools, mixed environments — one extension covers it.

Students don't think about it

Register once, enter a class code, forget it exists. The extension runs silently in the background. No pop-ups, no interruptions during writing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

Does Veratype read what students type?
No. Veratype never stores or reads student writing. It analyzes behavioural patterns — keystroke timing, paste events, editing flow. Key-pair rhythm is stored only as irreversible hashes. The actual words never leave the browser.
How does Veratype detect AI-generated writing?
Veratype scores 18 behavioural signals: paste ratio, typing rhythm variance, typing-rhythm baselines, editing distribution, session duration, and more.
What platforms does Veratype support?
Veratype works in Google Docs and Word Online (including word.office.com, word.cloud.microsoft, SharePoint, and OneDrive) via a free Chrome extension.
Is Veratype free?
Yes. The Chrome extension is free for students and educators. School-wide and district licensing is available — contact us for details.

Ready to see writing behaviour data?

Free for educators. One-click install. No setup for students.
